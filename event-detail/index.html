About this Event

TechFest 2026 is the flagship annual hackathon of our college, bringing together the brightest minds from across the country. Over 48 electrifying hours, teams will ideate, build, and present solutions to real-world problems. Mentors from top tech companies, workshops, and networking sessions make this the ultimate tech experience.

Rules & Guidelines

  • ✓ Teams of 2–4 members. Solo participation not allowed.
  • ✓ All code must be written during the event. Pre-built solutions are disqualified.
  • ✓ Use of open-source libraries and APIs is permitted.
  • ✓ Projects must be original and not previously submitted elsewhere.
  • ✓ Participants must be available for Q&A during judging.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?
Any student from any college with a valid student ID. Teams of 2–4 members.
Is registration free?
Yes, registration is completely free for all students.
What should I bring?
Your laptop, charger, and a government-issued ID or college ID card.
Will food be provided?
Yes, meals and snacks will be provided throughout the 48-hour event.
